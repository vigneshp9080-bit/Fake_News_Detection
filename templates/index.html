Process

How It Works

A three-stage deep learning pipeline processes your article from raw text to verified verdict.

01

Text Tokenization

Your article is broken into subword tokens using the DistilBERT tokenizer. Special [CLS] and [SEP] tokens are injected for classification.

BERT Tokenizer
02

Transformer Analysis

6-layer DistilBERT model runs bidirectional self-attention across all tokens, building deep contextual understanding of the article.

DistilBERT v2
03

Verdict Classification

A softmax classifier outputs probabilities for Real vs Fake. The class with highest logit score becomes the final verdict.

Softmax Output

About The Model

Built on DistilBERT

TruthScan uses a fine-tuned DistilBERT model — a compressed version of Google's BERT that retains 97% of the language understanding capability at 60% of the size and 40% faster inference speed.

The model was trained on the Indian Fake News corpus — a curated dataset of verified real and fake news articles sourced from reputable fact-checking organizations and authentic Indian media outlets.
